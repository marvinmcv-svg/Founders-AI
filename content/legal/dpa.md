# Data processing addendum

## Scope
This addendum applies where {entity} processes personal data on behalf of a customer (for example CRM records a customer uploads or syncs).

## Roles
The customer is the controller of customer-provided data; {entity} is its processor. For the professional profile database, {entity} is an independent controller.

## Security
We apply access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging and tenant isolation. Sub-processors are listed on request and notified in advance of changes.

## International transfers
Where data leaves its origin jurisdiction we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Deletion
On termination we delete or return customer data within 30 days unless law requires otherwise.
