## Data we process about our customers and visitors
Account data (name, work email, workspace), usage data inside the product, and — only with your consent — analytics cookies on this website. Demo and partner form submissions are used to respond to you and are routed to our sales team.

## Data we process about professionals in our database
Our platform contains professional information about founders, executives and investors (for example name, current role, employer, career history and public professional profiles). We collect it from licensed providers and public sources, never from sites whose terms forbid it. We do not process special-category data.

## Lawful basis
For professional profile data we rely on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), documented in a Legitimate Interest Assessment. For customers we rely on contract. For analytics cookies we rely on consent.

## Your rights
You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your profile, and object to processing, at /privacy-rights. Deleted profiles are added to a suppression list so they are not re-created.

## Retention
We keep profile data while it remains accurate and relevant, and delete stale records on a schedule. Customer data is kept for the life of the contract plus legal retention periods.
