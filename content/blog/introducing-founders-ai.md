Founders started with a simple idea: turn builders into real founders. We run an early-stage program in Santa Cruz, Bolivia, with validation, pilot companies, mentors and investors, and we launched Founders Academy to help people shape their first pitch.

Running that program taught us something about this market. The information that decides who gets backed is scattered. Global databases stop at the US and Europe, update slowly, and miss most of what happens in Bolivia, Paraguay or Peru. The people who should meet each other often don't, because nobody can see them.

**Founders AI is our answer.** It is the intelligence engine of the Founders ecosystem.

## What it does

- **One workspace for the whole market.** {stats.companies} companies, {stats.people} people, {stats.investors} investors and {stats.transactions} transactions, connected and searchable.
- **Signals, not just records.** Talent signals flag operators who are about to build. News signals turn articles into one scored line. Revenue and valuation estimates come with honest ranges.
- **Ask in plain English.** Describe what you want and get filters you can edit, save and share.
- **An agent that does the legwork.** Run a skill like a company brief or a market map and get a sourced answer in minutes.
- **Plug it into your own tools.** {limits.endpoints} API endpoints and an MCP server with {limits.mcpTools} tools for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI clients.

## Built from Latin America out

We started with the ecosystems we know best and hold them to the same standard as anywhere else. That is the point: the next generation of founders in this region deserves to be seen.
